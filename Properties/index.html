How FoodBridge Works

A simple 4-step process ensuring surplus food reaches the right people at the right time.

01
Register & List

Donors register and post available leftover food with quantity, type, and pickup window.

02
NGO Reviews

Verified NGOs browse available donations and accept requests that match their capacity.

03
Volunteer Picks Up

Assigned volunteers are notified, pick up food from donor, and deliver to the NGO.

04
Tracked & Confirmed

Delivery is confirmed, feedback collected, and the transaction logged for full transparency.
