Process

How FoodBridge Works

A simple 4-step process ensuring surplus food reaches the right people at the right time.

01
Register & List

Donors register, wait for admin approval, then post surplus food with quantity, type, expiry and pickup window.

02
Approved & Claimed

An admin reviews the listing, then verified NGOs see it and claim the ones matching their capacity.

03
Volunteer Collects

An admin assigns a volunteer, who confirms pickup and delivery from their own dashboard.

04
Confirmed & Rated

The NGO confirms what arrived, everyone involved can rate each other, and the whole chain stays on record.

Features

Everything the Platform Does

Every item below is built and working in the application — nothing here is planned or coming soon.

Donation Posting

Food description, category, quantity, servings, expiry, dietary flags, pickup address and window, contact details and a photo upload.

Admin Approval

Accounts and donations are both reviewed before they go live. Admins approve, reject, suspend and reinstate from a single queue.

NGO Claiming

Verified NGOs browse approved donations and claim them. Claiming is race-safe, so two NGOs can never win the same donation.

Volunteer Assignment

Admins assign volunteers, seeing each one's current workload first. Volunteers confirm pickup and delivery as they go.

Maps & Live Tracking

Pickup and drop-off plotted on OpenStreetMap, with optional live volunteer location during an active delivery — opt-in, and off by default.

Donation Tracking

Donors follow a five-step timeline — posted, approved, accepted, picked up, delivered — with real timestamps and a live expiry countdown.

Notifications

Eight events raise an in-app notification and an email, with per-event preferences. Account and security messages are always delivered.

Ratings & Trust

Donor, NGO and volunteer rate each other after a completed delivery. Scores roll up into a trust level computed from real deliveries.

Emergency Mode

Admins declare an emergency and broadcast to a targeted audience by role and city, with the recipient count shown before sending.

Fraud Detection

Five rules flag duplicate listings, rapid posting, repeated cancellations, quantity mismatches and unverifiable addresses — into a review queue, never an automatic block.

Reports & Analytics

Period-scoped figures on donations, meals, fulfilment and waste, with breakdowns by category and city, CSV export and a print-to-PDF layout.

Role-Scoped Search

One search box across the app that only ever returns what your role is entitled to see — your own donations, the ones open to you, or everything if you administer it.
